## What This App Covers

Jonaki Learn is where students choose a direction. After completing Jonaki Kids (or entering via placement assessment), students pick one or two pathways from six and follow them to professional readiness. Students may show complete projects and practice industry simulation to be ready for read world. 

**Framework alignment:** Cambridge Lower Secondary + Cambridge IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education)
**Entry:** Via placement assessment or completion of Jonaki Kids Grade 5
**Languages:** Bengali primary + English mandatory throughout

---

## The Journey

```
Placement Assessment
        ↓
★ CHOICE POINT 1 — Which pathway(s)?
        ↓
Pathway Levels 1–5 (one or two pathways)
        ↓
★ CHOICE POINT 2 — Which direction?
  Self-Employment · Job-Seeking · Entrepreneurship
        ↓
Graduate → Jonaki Skills (App 3) or real-world readiness
```

Students may choose **one or two pathways.** Completing two unlocks a merged specialisation at graduation.

---

## Placement Assessment — Jonaki Learn Specific

Full design in [ASSESSMENT.md](ASSESSMENT.md). Summary:

**3 rounds of adaptive questions before pathway selection:**
- Round 1 → Pathway Level 1 entry (literacy + numeracy baseline)
- Round 2 → Pathway Level 2 entry (comprehension + reasoning)
- Round 3 → Pathway Level 3 entry (analysis + applied thinking)

**After general placement:** Students indicate their pathway preference. A short 5-question pathway-specific diagnostic helps the app flag any foundational gaps — but does not gatekeep. Students choose their own entry point.

**Mastery threshold:** 70% per unit. Unlimited retakes. No time limits. See ASSESSMENT.md for full logic.

## Structure of Every Pathway

Each pathway has **5 levels.** Each level has:
- Core skills for the field
- Ethics specific to that field
- AI tools relevant to that field
- Multiple income streams in that field
- Real portfolio work produced

Level 5 of every pathway includes full preparation for all three career directions.

## 🔧 Technical Pathway
*Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science (0984) aligned*

**Level 1 — How Technology Works**
- How computers process instructions
- Hardware vs software
- How the internet works: packets, servers, browsers
- Binary and data basics
- History of technology — who built what and who is left out
- Why open source matters
- *Ethics:* Who owns technology? Who benefits, who is excluded?
- *AI in technical work:* What AI tools do now, what they cannot do

**Level 2 — Logical Thinking**
- Algorithmic thinking: steps to solve a problem
- Flowcharts
- Breaking complex problems into manageable parts
- Debugging: finding and fixing errors
- Logical operators: AND, OR, NOT
- Introduction to data structures
- *IGCSE alignment:* Problem solving and algorithm design unit

**Level 3 — First Code**
- Scratch: visual programming to learn logic without syntax
- Python fundamentals: variables, loops, conditions, functions
- Every concept built into something that actually runs
- Code review and documentation
- *IGCSE alignment:* Programming concepts unit

**Level 4 — Web and Applications**
- HTML structure
- CSS styling
- JavaScript basics
- What no-code tools can and cannot do
- Building a working mobile-first webpage
- API basics: how applications communicate
- *Portfolio work:* A real working webpage, deployed

**Level 5 — Building Real Things**
- Complete project: brief to deployment
- Database concepts
- User experience design basics
- Testing with real users
- Documentation for other developers
- Open source contribution: first pull request
- *Career directions:* Freelance developer · Junior developer employment · Tech startup

**Assessment per level:** 70% mastery threshold. Practical project component required for Level 4 and 5 certificates.

## 💼 Business Pathway
*Cambridge IGCSE Business Studies (0450) aligned*

**Level 1 — Money and Mind**
- Financial psychology: why people make poor financial decisions
- Personal budgeting: the system that actually works
- Systematic saving as non-negotiable
- Understanding debt: mechanics, psychology, trap
- Credit: how it works, when it helps
- *IGCSE alignment:* Business activity and enterprise unit

**Level 2 — Finding the Opportunity**
- Identifying real problems worth solving
- Market research without spending money
- Idea validation: asking real people before building
- What customers actually want vs what they say they want
- Minimum viable product: the smallest version that tests an idea
- Why most businesses fail — and how to prevent it
- *Ethics:* Identifying opportunities vs exploiting vulnerabilities
- *IGCSE alignment:* Marketing and market research unit

**Level 3 — Building the Business**
- Legal registration basics (localised per language version)
- Pricing: how to charge what work is worth
- Finding first customers without a budget
- Sales without manipulation
- Customer service as competitive advantage
- Basic bookkeeping
- *IGCSE alignment:* Financial information and decisions unit

**Level 4 — Digital Business**
- Online selling platforms
- Digital marketing fundamentals
- Email marketing: highest ROI channel explained
- Social media for business: what produces results vs what wastes time
- Free tools to run a lean business
- Invoicing and receiving international payments
- Passive income through digital products
- *AI in business:* Automation tools, what they replace and what they don't
- *Portfolio work:* A complete business plan for a real idea

**Level 5 — Growth and Sustainability**
- When and how to hire the first person
- Managing cash flow
- Building systems that work without you
- Long-term planning
- Community responsibility as a business grows
- *Career directions:* Micro-business owner · Business employee · Entrepreneur

## 🎨 Creative Pathway
*Cambridge IGCSE Art & Design (0400) + Media Studies (0411) aligned*

**Level 1 — Design Foundations**
- Colour theory: why colours affect people the way they do
- Typography: how type choices communicate before a word is read
- Composition and visual hierarchy
- The design process: brief → research → ideas → execution → feedback → iteration
- What makes communication clear vs confusing
- How to give and receive critique

**Level 2 — Digital Design**
- Figma / Penpot fundamentals
- Canva for production work
- Designing for different contexts: social, print, web, mobile
- Brand identity: logo, colour, typography, tone
- Design systems: consistency at scale
- Accessibility in design: designing for different visual abilities
- *AI in creative work:* What AI image tools do, what they can't replace
- *Ethics:* Copyright from a creator's perspective

**Level 3 — Motion and Video**
- Video storytelling: what makes images communicate a narrative
- Editing with free tools
- Lottie animation fundamentals
- Short-form content creation
- Sound design basics
- *IGCSE alignment:* Moving image production unit

**Level 4 — Illustration and Interactive Design**
- Digital illustration techniques
- Character design
- Rive interactive animation
- UI/UX design: designing experiences, not just visuals
- What makes a portfolio professional
- *Multiple income streams:* Client work · selling digital assets · teaching · licensing

**Level 5 — Creative Business**
- Pricing creative work honestly
- Licensing vs selling outright
- Building an audience without burning out
- Pitching to clients
- Artist statement
- Long-term creative practice: sustainability
- *Career directions:* Freelance designer · Agency/studio employment · Creative studio founder
- *Portfolio work:* Complete brand identity project + motion samples

## 🏥 Health Pathway
*Cambridge IGCSE Biology (0610) aligned + Community Health framework*

**Level 1 — The Human Body**
- Major body systems and how they interact
- Disease: what it is, how it spreads, how immunity works
- Genetics basics: traits and heredity
- Mental and physical health as inseparable
- Social determinants of health: poverty, housing, food security
- *IGCSE alignment:* Organism and life processes unit
- *Ethics:* Patient dignity · Informed consent · Honesty about what you don't know

**Level 2 — Nutrition and Prevention**
- Macronutrients and micronutrients
- Both types of malnutrition: deficiency and excess
- Food security: why some communities lack nutritious food
- Vaccination science: how it works, the evidence, the misinformation
- Hygiene at community scale
- Non-communicable disease prevention
- *IGCSE alignment:* Health, disease and medicine unit

**Level 3 — First Aid and Emergency Response**
- CPR and choking
- Bleeding control and burns
- Fractures and sprains
- Poisoning and anaphylaxis
- Maternal emergencies
- Psychological first aid
- When to refer and when to act
- *Aligned to:* International Red Cross first aid standards
- *Expert review required before deployment — see CHILD_SAFETY_PSYCHOLOGY.md*

**Level 4 — Mental Health Literacy**
- Mental health conditions explained without stigma
- Depression, anxiety, PTSD, psychosis: what they are
- Supporting someone in mental health crisis
- Active listening as a learnable skill
- Self-care for helpers: vicarious trauma and burnout
- Recognising burnout in yourself

**Level 5 — Community Health Systems**
- Disease at population level
- Community health worker role
- Health record keeping
- Health advocacy: influencing decisions from community level
- Climate change and health: expanding disease ranges, extreme heat, food insecurity
- *Career directions:* Private first aid trainer · Community health worker · NGO health role

## 🌱 Agriculture Pathway
*Cambridge IGCSE Environmental Management (0680) aligned*

**Level 1 — Soil, Seeds, and Systems**
- Soil science: what makes soil productive and what degrades it
- Plant biology: how plants grow
- Seed selection and seed saving
- Crop rotation: the soil science behind it
- Companion planting
- Local climate and microclimate
- *IGCSE alignment:* Ecosystems and environmental management unit
- *Ethics:* Land rights · Seed ownership · Fair trade from the farmer's perspective

**Level 2 — Water and Climate Resilience**
- Irrigation methods and their real costs
- Rainwater harvesting
- Drought-resistant varieties
- Flood management
- Climate-smart farming: not optional for farmers in vulnerable regions
- Reading weather patterns and long-term climate trends
- *Biodiversity in agriculture:* Why diverse farms are more resilient than monocultures

**Level 3 — Modern and Sustainable Techniques**
- Organic farming: methods and economics
- Composting and soil fertility
- Biological pest control
- Greenhouse and protected growing
- Hydroponics basics
- Reducing chemical dependency

**Level 4 — The Business of Farming**
- Market access: farmers as the weakest party in the supply chain
- Price negotiation
- Storage and preservation
- Cooperatives and collective bargaining
- Microfinance for farmers
- Selling directly vs through middlemen
- *Multiple income streams:* Primary crop + secondary + agri-tourism + value-added products

**Level 5 — Future Agriculture**
- Food security as global challenge and local opportunity
- Agroforestry: integrating trees and crops
- Urban and peri-urban farming
- Climate change adaptation: specific to each language version's region
- Agricultural advocacy
- *Career directions:* Profitable small farm · Agricultural technician · Agricultural enterprise founder

## 👩‍🏫 Education Pathway
*Cambridge IGCSE Sociology (0495) aligned + Education Theory*

**Level 1 — How People Learn**
- Brain development from infancy through adulthood
- What the evidence actually says about learning styles
- Attention and working memory
- Play-based learning: why it works
- The role of emotion in memory
- Why traditional education systems fail many learners
- *IGCSE alignment:* Socialisation and education unit
- *Ethics:* The educator's responsibility not to create compliance

**Level 2 — Teaching Methods**
- Lesson planning starting from what the learner needs
- Bloom's Taxonomy: questions that develop thinking vs questions that test recall
- Managing a learning group through relationship not punishment
- Inclusive education: designing for diverse learners
- Formative assessment: checking understanding without creating anxiety
- Feedback that improves learning rather than damages confidence
- *Creativity in education:* Open-ended questions, problems with multiple answers

**Level 3 — Child and Adolescent Development**
- Piaget's developmental stages: what children can do at what ages
- Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development
- Attachment theory basics
- Trauma-informed practice
- Supporting neurodivergent learners without clinical support
- Recognising signs of abuse and neglect
- *Expert review required — see CHILD_SAFETY_PSYCHOLOGY.md*

**Level 4 — Community and Adult Education**
- How adults learn differently from children
- Non-formal education
- Running community learning groups with mixed ages
- Peer teaching: how to structure it so it works
- Facilitating difficult community conversations
- How to run Jonaki-based learning groups without internet

**Level 5 — Education Systems and Leadership**
- Curriculum design principles
- Measuring learning outcomes honestly
- Education advocacy: influencing decisions in your community
- Building an education programme from scratch
- Grant writing for education projects
- Climate and sustainability education: producing agency not paralysis
- *Career directions:* Private tutor · Community education worker · Education centre founder

## Certification — Jonaki Learn

**Level certificate issued when:**
- All units in that pathway level completed
- 70% mastery threshold met per unit
- Portfolio work submitted and reviewed (Levels 4 and 5)

**Certificate states:**
*"This certifies that [pseudonym] has completed Jonaki Learn [Pathway Name] Level [X], aligned with Cambridge IGCSE [subject name]."*

**Graduation certificate (full pathway):**
Issued on completion of all 5 levels. Includes full transcript of units, mastery scores, and portfolio work reference.

**Dual pathway graduation:**
Students who complete two pathways receive a Merged Specialisation certificate naming both pathways and the combined specialisation title.

All certificates: blockchain-verifiable, pseudonymous, permanent.
