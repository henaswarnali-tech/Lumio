## What This App Covers

Jonaki Skills is for working adults who need specific, verifiable professional skills — fast. Courses are short (10–25 hours), practical, and immediately applicable. Every course ends with a blockchain-verified certificate that an employer can check on their phone in seconds.

**Framework alignment:** Edexcel BTEC Level 2 and Level 3 (vocational qualifications recognised globally)
**Entry:** Any adult. Self-declared experience level. Short diagnostic confirms entry point.
**Languages:** Bengali primary + English professional communication throughout

---

## What Edexcel BTEC Means for Students

BTEC (Business and Technology Education Council) is run by Pearson/Edexcel — the UK's leading vocational qualification body, recognised by employers and universities in 100+ countries.

- BTEC Level 2 = equivalent to GCSE / O Level
- BTEC Level 3 = equivalent to A Level

Jonaki Skills certificates state alignment with BTEC standards. This means an employer anywhere in the world understands what level the student has reached.

*Note: Jonaki is aligned with BTEC, not formally accredited by it. Formal accreditation is a future milestone pending scale and funding.*

## Placement Assessment — Jonaki Skills Specific

Full design in [ASSESSMENT.md](ASSESSMENT.md). Summary for Skills:

**Self-declared entry (3 options):**
1. No experience in this field → starts at beginning of course
2. Some informal experience → 5-question diagnostic, placed at appropriate module
3. Have worked in this field, want formal recognition → diagnostic + fast-track option

**No gatekeeping.** If a student wants to start at the beginning, they can. If they want to skip ahead, they take the diagnostic and the app places them appropriately.

**Mastery threshold:** 70% per module. Unlimited retakes. No time limits.

**Portfolio requirement:** Courses at Level 3 equivalent require one piece of practical work as part of completion. Theory alone is not enough.

## Certification — Jonaki Skills

**Course certificate issued when:**
- All modules in the course completed
- 70% mastery threshold met per module
- Portfolio work submitted (for Level 3 equivalent courses)

**Certificate states:**
*"This certifies that [pseudonym] has completed [Course Name], aligned with [BTEC Level X, Unit X: Subject Name] / [Cambridge qualification name]."*

**The certificate includes:**
- Course name and duration
- Framework alignment level
- Modules completed and mastery scores
- Portfolio work completed (title and type)
- Unique certificate ID
- QR code for blockchain verification

All certificates: blockchain-verifiable, pseudonymous, permanent. Employer scans QR → sees verified certificate page in seconds.
