# Jonaki — Assessment Design
### Applies to all three apps: Jonaki Kids · Jonaki Learn · Jonaki Skills

---

## Core Philosophy

Assessment in Jonaki has one job: **find where the student is and help them move forward.**

It is never used to rank, compare, judge, or label a student. No student ever sees a score, a percentage, or a grade letter as a result of assessment. They see a starting point on a map — and the first lesson waiting for them.

The word **"wrong"** does not appear anywhere in the app.
The word **"failed"** does not appear anywhere in the app.
There are no time limits on any assessment.
There is no limit on retakes.

---

## Part 1 — Placement Assessment (Entry Point)

### What it is

A 5-minute adaptive test that runs once when a student first opens the app. It finds the right starting point so a student who already knows Grade 3 content doesn't sit through Grades 1 and 2.

### Who it's for

- A child who has been to school but dropped out
- An adult entering with some prior knowledge
- A student who learned informally and has gaps
- A complete beginner — they are placed at Grade 1 and that is completely fine

### How it works

**5 rounds. Each round has 3 questions.**

Rule: Get 2 out of 3 correct → advance to the next round.
Get fewer than 2 out of 3 → placed at the start of that round's grade level.

The test stops as soon as a student's level is found. A student who passes all 5 rounds starts at Grade 5.

---

### Jonaki Kids — Placement Rounds

**Round 1 — Zero literacy entry → Grade 1 if stopped here**
- Tap the picture of the cat (image identification, no reading)
- Count 3 dots (visual counting)
- Find the circle (shape recognition)
- *All images, no text required*

**Round 2 → Grade 2 if stopped here**
- Match a letter to its picture (letter recognition)
- Which picture shows the word "বাড়ি" (word-image matching)
- 3 + 4 = ? (simple addition with images)

**Round 3 → Grade 3 if stopped here**
- Read a short sentence and answer a question about it
- 6 × 7 = ? (multiplication)
- Which is a living thing? (concept question with images)

**Round 4 → Grade 4 if stopped here**
- Read a short paragraph and identify the main idea
- What is ¾ of 20? (fractions)
- Name a country in South Asia (geography)

**Round 5 → Grade 5 if stopped here (or passes all 5 → Grade 5)**
- Read a short argument and identify the logical error
- What is 15% of 200? (percentage)
- A student claims the Earth is flat because "it looks flat." What type of error is this? (critical thinking)

---

### Jonaki Learn — Placement Rounds

Students entering Jonaki Learn may come from:
- Completing Jonaki Kids Grade 5
- External education (school, madrasa, etc.)
- Self-study

**Round 1 → Pathway Level 1 if stopped here**
- Basic numeracy and literacy check (same as Kids Round 3–4)
- Reading comprehension: short paragraph
- Simple reasoning question

**Round 2 → Pathway Level 2 if stopped here**
- Vocabulary and language use
- Proportional reasoning
- Basic scientific concept

**Round 3 → Pathway Level 3 if stopped here**
- Analysis question: interpret a graph or data set
- Extended reading: 150-word passage with inference question
- Applied maths: real-world problem

**Pathway placement:** After the general placement, students also indicate their pathway preference. Pathway-specific pre-assessment is optional and advisory — it helps the app highlight areas to revisit, not gatekeep.

---

### Jonaki Skills — Placement Rounds

Students entering Jonaki Skills are adults seeking professional skills. Placement is simpler:

**Self-declared entry:**
Students indicate their background:
- No experience in this field
- Some informal experience
- Have worked in this field, want formal recognition

The app adjusts the course starting point based on self-declaration, then runs a short 5-question diagnostic per course to confirm. No gatekeeping — if a student wants to start at Level 1, they can.

## Part 2 — In-Course Mastery Assessment

### The threshold

**70% correct = mastery = advance to next unit.**

This number is not arbitrary. It represents genuine demonstrated understanding — not perfect knowledge, but real capability. A student who passes at 70% has understood the core concepts. A student at 69% needs a little more time with the content.

### What happens when mastery is not reached

1. The student sees which specific concept needs more practice (not a total score)
2. The relevant lesson content replays automatically
3. The student can attempt the assessment again — immediately, no waiting
4. This cycle repeats until mastery is achieved
5. There is no limit on how many times this cycle runs

### What the student sees

Not: "You scored 60%. You failed."

Instead: "Let's look at fractions one more time — that part needs a bit more practice."

Then the mascot plays the Encouraging animation. The student reviews. They try again.

### Feedback language — required in all assessment UI

| Situation | What the app says | What the app never says |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Correct answer | "দারুণ!" / "Yes!" / "Exactly right" | "Correct" (too clinical) |
| Wrong answer | "আবার দেখি" / "Let's look again" | "Wrong" "Incorrect" "Try again" (punitive) |
| Mastery achieved | "তুমি এটা শিখে ফেলেছ!" / "You've got it!" | Score percentage |
| Mastery not yet | "এই অংশটা আর একটু দেখি" | "Failed" "Not enough" |

## Part 3 — Progress Visibility

**What students see:**
- Their character moving forward on the learning map
- Completed nodes shown in full colour
- Current lesson glowing and pulsing
- Locked future content greyed out (waiting, not blocked)

**What students never see:**
- Comparison with other students
- Class rankings or leaderboards
- A percentage score on any assessment
- How many attempts they've taken

**What the app tracks internally (for certificates only):**
- Mastery scores per unit
- Date of completion
- Number of sessions (not number of attempts — that would shame)

---

## Part 4 — Re-Placement

A student can request re-assessment at any time from Settings.

Reasons this matters:
- A student might have been placed too low and find the content too easy
- A student might have rushed through placement
- A returning student after a long break might want to confirm their level

Re-placement follows the same 5-round adaptive test. Progress already completed is never deleted — the student simply picks a new starting point if they advance.

---

## Part 5 — Certificate Issuance

Certificates are issued when:
1. All units in a grade/pathway level are complete
2. Each unit has reached the 70% mastery threshold
3. The student chooses to claim their certificate (it is never forced on them)

The certificate records:
- Student pseudonym (never real name)
- Grade or pathway level completed
- Each unit and its mastery score
- Date issued
- A unique certificate ID for blockchain verification

**No certificate is issued for partial completion.** A student who completes 4 out of 5 units gets encouragement and a progress summary — not a certificate. The certificate means something because it requires genuine completion.
