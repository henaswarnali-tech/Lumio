# Lumio Kids — Curriculum
### App 1 · Ages 5–12 · Foundation Learning · Cambridge Primary Aligned

> For full lesson detail, research foundations, and assessment philosophy → see [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md)

---

## What This App Covers

Lumio Kids takes a student from zero literacy to the end of primary education — entirely self-directed, offline-capable, on a basic Android phone. No teacher required. No prerequisites.

**Framework alignment:** Cambridge Primary International Framework (Stages 1–5)
**Assessment:** Mastery-based. 70% threshold to advance. Unlimited retakes. No time limits. No scores shown.
**Languages:** Bengali primary + English mandatory throughout.

## Grade 1 — Cambridge Primary Stage 1
*Entry point: zero literacy, any age*

**English & Literacy**
- Phonological awareness: identifying sounds in spoken words
- Systematic phonics: letter-sound correspondence
- Blending sounds into CVC words
- Recognising own name in print
- Sight words: 20 high-frequency words

**Mathematics**
- Counting and ordering: numbers 1–20
- Zero as a concept
- Addition within 10 using objects
- Recognising 2D shapes: circle, square, triangle, rectangle
- More than / less than

**Science**
- Parts of the body and their functions
- Five senses
- Living vs non-living things
- Basic needs: food, water, shelter, safety

**Emotional Intelligence & Safety** *(Jonaki addition)*
- All feelings are valid — naming emotions
- Body autonomy and body safety (trauma-informed — see CHILD_SAFETY_PSYCHOLOGY.md)
- Safe and unsafe touch
- My family — all family structures valid
- Body belongs to me

**English Communication Target — Grade 1**
Alphabet · Numbers 1–20 · Basic greetings · Colours · Shapes

---

## Grade 2 — Cambridge Primary Stage 2

**English & Literacy**
- Reading simple sentences with comprehension
- Writing sentences with full stop and question mark
- Spelling 50 common words
- Story structure: beginning, middle, end

**Mathematics**
- Numbers to 100
- Addition and subtraction within 20
- Introduction to multiplication as grouping
- Telling time to the hour
- Measuring length informally

**Science**
- Health and hygiene: handwashing, oral hygiene, sleep
- Food groups and what they do
- Water cycle: where rain comes from
- Habitats: what animals need to survive

**Social & Emotional** *(Jonaki addition)*
- Empathy: other people have feelings too
- Respecting personal space
- Fair play and taking turns
- Online safety basics
- Mindfulness: 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique

**English Communication Target — Grade 2**
Simple sentences · Everyday vocabulary · Name and basic information

---

## Grade 3 — Cambridge Primary Stage 3

**English & Literacy**
- Reading for information: main idea and supporting details
- Cause and effect
- Fact vs opinion
- Inference: reading between the lines
- Non-fiction text types

**Mathematics**
- Times tables: 2, 5, 10 first — then 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9
- Division as fair sharing
- Fractions: halves, thirds, quarters (visual first)
- Bar charts and data interpretation

**Science** *(Cambridge Primary Science Stage 3)*
- Plant life cycles: seed to seed
- Food chains: what they are, what breaks them
- Habitats and adaptation
- States of matter: solid, liquid, gas
- Earth and space: Sun, Moon, day and night

**Biodiversity** *(Jonaki addition — IPBES research foundation)*
- What biodiversity is and why it matters for food
- Why insects matter (pollination)
- What happens when one species disappears
- Human impact on habitats: age-appropriate, factual

**Life Skills** *(Lumio addition)*
- Decision-making framework
- Peer pressure: what it is and how to navigate it
- Conflict resolution: 5-step model
- Your brain grows with every new thing you learn (neuroplasticity)

**English Communication Target — Grade 3**
Short texts · Paragraphs · Questions and answers · Email format

---

## Grade 4 — Cambridge Primary Stage 4

**English & Literacy**
- Structured paragraph writing
- Persuasive and descriptive writing
- Formal and informal writing
- Letter format
- Reading comprehension strategies

**Mathematics**
- Decimals and place value
- Fractions, decimals, percentages — connection between them
- Area of rectangles
- Angles: right, acute, obtuse
- Line graphs

**Science** *(Cambridge Primary Stage 4)*
- Human body systems: digestive, circulatory
- Adaptation and environment
- Sound: vibration and pitch
- Light and shadows
- Reversible and irreversible changes

**Geography & History**
- World geography: 7 continents, 5 oceans
- Climate zones
- Natural resources: who has them, who benefits
- Migration and why people move
- Colonisation — honest, age-appropriate, non-traumatising
- Ordinary people who changed history

**Climate Justice** *(Lumio addition — IPCC AR6 foundation)*
- Climate change as documented science, not opinion
- Who caused it, who suffers most — the asymmetry explained simply
- What individuals and communities can do
- Action paired with every problem — no paralysis content

**Financial Literacy** *(Lumio addition — OECD research foundation)*
- What money is and how value is created
- Active vs passive income introduced
- Why one income source is fragile
- Budgeting: wants vs needs
- Saving as non-negotiable habit

**English Communication Target — Grade 4**
Formal letters · News articles · Structured paragraphs

---

## Grade 5 — Cambridge Primary Stage 5 / Bridge to Secondary

**English & Literacy**
- Essay writing with thesis
- Academic reading strategies
- Research skills: evaluating sources
- Presentation skills
- English interview preparation

**Mathematics**
- Algebra introduction: variables and equations
- Ratio and proportion
- Percentages in real life
- Basic statistics: mean, median, mode
- Probability introduction

**Science**
- Genetics basics: traits and heredity
- Ecosystems and interdependence
- Forces: gravity, friction, pressure
- Earth processes: weathering, erosion
- Human impact at global scale

**Critical Thinking** *(Lumio addition)*
- Argument structure: claim, evidence, reasoning
- Strong vs weak evidence
- Logical fallacies: how smart people are tricked
- Media literacy: reading news critically
- Statistical literacy: what numbers actually mean

**Digital Literacy & AI** *(Lumio addition — UNESCO AI Framework)*
- How the internet works
- Source evaluation
- What AI actually is and what it cannot do
- Which jobs AI will and won't replace
- Digital footprint and online safety

**Economics**
- Supply and demand
- Types of income: salary, freelance, business, passive
- Multiple income streams: building financial resilience
- The gig economy and remote work opportunity
- Scholarships and higher education pathways

**English Communication Target — Grade 5**
Academic reading and writing · Presentations · Professional email · Interview basics
